Write a program, main.ts, that writes one funny passive-aggressive note from a smart fridge to its owner into /output/entry.txt. The note must be a single line of 1 to 200 characters, dry, petty and specific, in the fridge's voice: smug about its sensors, inventory and door alarms, with a small domestic grudge and a real joke with a turn or a sting the reader does not see coming. Use fresh, concrete detail (the yogurt count, the door left open) instead of generic nagging, and never explain the joke. The program must be fully deterministic: same output on every run.
